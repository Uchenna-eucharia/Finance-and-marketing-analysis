# Marketing and Finance Analysis

### Project Overview
This project analyzes sales data using Microsoft Excel to evaluate business performance across products, customer segments, regions, locations, and time. An interactive dashboard was developed to help business owners, finance managers, and marketing managers make data-driven decisions.
![original](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/6ec8f126-187d-4079-a9d3-1de4d2dcddad)

### Problem Statement
The business had a large volume of sales data but needed a simple way to identify sales trends, high-performing products, customer segments, and regions. The analysis provides management with a clear view of performance and areas requiring attention.

### Data Source
The dataset contains 9,995 rows and 23 columns, covering sales transactions, products, customers, regions, locations, quantities, orders, and discounts.

### Methodology
The analysis was conducted using Microsoft Excel through the following steps:
Data cleaning and preparation
Creation of calculated KPIs and metrics
Total Revenue analysis
Profit Generated analysis
Total Orders analysis
Average Discount analysis
Total Quantity analysis
Monthly Sales Trend analysis
Product Category analysis
Customer Segmentation analysis
Revenue Year-over-Year (YoY) comparison
Quantity Year-over-Year (YoY) comparison
Total Orders Year-over-Year (YoY) comparison
Discount Year-over-Year (YoY) comparison
Location Summary analysis
Dashboard design and data visualization to communicate insights 

### EXCEL & DATA ANALYTICS SKILLS
* Data Analysis: Data cleaning, transformation, KPI development, YoY analysis
* DAX: Calculated measures and business metrics
* Excel: Advanced formulas, Pivot Tables, dynamic charts
* Visualization: Slicers, filters, conditional formatting, dashboard design
* Business Intelligence: Trend analysis, customer segmentation, product and location analysis

### Key Insights
* The business generated $2.30M in revenue from 37,873 units and 5,009 orders, with an average discount of 15.62%.
* Technology was the highest-performing product category, generating $836.15K, followed by Furniture ($742.00K) and Office Supplies ($729.05K).
* Consumer customers generated the highest revenue at approximately $1.16M, followed by Corporate ($705.15K) and Home Office ($429.56K).
* California was the highest-performing location at approximately $397.69K, followed by New York ($310.88K), Texas ($170.19K), Washington ($138.64K), and Pennsylvania ($116.51K).
* Revenue showed noticeable monthly fluctuations, with November recording the strongest performance while February recorded the lowest.
* YoY analysis shows changes in revenue, quantity, orders, and discount, enabling management to track business growth and identify areas of improvement.
* The 15.62% average discount highlights the need to monitor discounting alongside revenue and profit to ensure that sales growth translates into profitable growth.

### Recommendation
Increase Technology revenue by 10% within 12 months, grow Consumer-segment revenue by 8%, and target a 10% increase in sales across high-performing locations such as California and New York. Reduce average discount from 15.62% to below 14% within six months while monitoring its impact on profitability. Management should also review YoY KPIs quarterly to identify performance gaps and implement corrective actions.
